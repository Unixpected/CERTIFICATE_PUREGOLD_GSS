PWD / SC Report Consolidator

Drop in your terminal-level PWD and Senior Citizen sales reports for one store and one period. This merges every terminal into a single workbook — one sheet for PWD, one for Senior Citizen — sorted by date, with a totals line.

I. Add your files

Accepts the _pwd_report.xls and _sc_report.xls files exported from the POS, any number of terminals, one store at a time. Files are read entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
